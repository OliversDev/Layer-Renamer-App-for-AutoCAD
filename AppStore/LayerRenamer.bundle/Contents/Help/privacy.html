Layer Renamer Privacy Policy

Publisher: Oliver Wackenreuther
Effective date: September 2, 2026

1. Application data

Layer Renamer does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information, drawing information, layer names, usage information, or analytics. It reads layer information from the active AutoCAD drawing and processes rename instructions locally solely to perform the operation requested by the user.

The application does not require a Publisher account and does not use telemetry, advertising, tracking technologies, cloud processing, or third-party SDKs that receive application data.

2. Local storage

Layer Renamer does not create application logs or retain drawing or layer information outside the active AutoCAD drawing.

3. Third parties and external links

Layer Renamer does not share application data with third parties. Optional GitHub and LinkedIn links are opened only when selected, and Layer Renamer does not send drawing or layer information to those sites. Their own privacy policies apply.

Information independently processed by Autodesk in connection with Marketplace access, downloads, accounts, or AutoCAD is governed by Autodesk's privacy policies and is not received through Layer Renamer. If a future version introduces a third party that receives application data, this policy will be updated before release and that party will be required to protect the data consistently with this policy and applicable law.

4. Support communications

If a user voluntarily contacts the Publisher, the Publisher may receive information the user chooses to provide. It will be used only to respond to or diagnose the request, retained only as reasonably necessary, and not sold or used for advertising. Users should remove confidential information before providing support materials.

Support information may be shared with a service provider only when necessary to deliver support or when required by law. Any provider will be expected to protect the information consistently with this policy.

5. Retention, deletion, and consent

There is no remotely stored application data for the Publisher to delete. A user may withdraw consent or request deletion of personal information voluntarily provided for support by using the support contact on the Autodesk Marketplace listing. Information may be retained when required by law or another legitimate purpose.

6. Security and children

Reasonable measures are used to protect information voluntarily submitted for support, but no transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Layer Renamer is a professional productivity tool and is not directed toward children.

7. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated before Layer Renamer begins collecting or transmitting application data. Privacy questions may be submitted through the Publisher support contact shown on the Layer Renamer Autodesk Marketplace listing.
